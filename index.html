Why Futobi

the name

When I went out on my own, I named the company for where I wanted to work: the future of business intelligence. I was trying to be forward thinking. The work then was data visualization, how data is presented, and how it's structured so it can answer questions as a story.

Somewhere along the way I moved down to the basement: pipelines, warehouses and the data preparation that feeds every report. It's been fun work, and it taught me a lot. Futobi Labs is me coming back up to the front end on my own time, bringing all of it with me.

The question hasn't changed. Everything we built to answer it is changing.
